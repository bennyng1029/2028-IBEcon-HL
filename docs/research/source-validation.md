# Source validation and assessment evidence

Research date: 14 November 2026. This is an independent check of `curriculum.md`, not a claim of IB endorsement or a complete item-by-item audit against the latest school copy.

## Guide and scope

- The [IB HL subject brief](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/hl-economics-en.pdf), pp. 1–2, confirms first assessment 2022, last assessment 2029, the topic map, teaching hours, and assessment components. It establishes the applicable published window for May 2028, subject to later IB notices.
- The [May 2020 guide copy](https://dp.uwcea.org/docs/Economics%20Subject%20Guide.pdf) identifies first assessment 2022. The [October 2022 updated copy](https://ibdocs.re/d/IB%20SUBJECT%20GUIDES/Group%203%20-%20Individuals%20and%20Societies/Economics/Economics%20Guide%202024%20-%20English%20(Update%20Oct2022).pdf) explicitly identifies first assessment 2024 on its cover and lists updates through October 2022 on its publication page; this is not inferred from its filename. Both are third-party-hosted; consult the school's current Programme Resource Centre copy for later corrections.
- A comparison of extracted text for Units 1–2 and assessment found the October 2022 guide labels all of 2.4 HL-only in its syllabus outline and detailed heading (printed pp. 20, 27); the earlier guide already labelled its component subtopics HL-only. The October 2022 guide changes HL Paper 2 to exclude HL extension material and states the paper is the same for SL and HL (pp. 58, 60–62); the May 2020 copy included HL extension material and allowed different papers. Assessment mark descriptors also changed in wording (pp. 64–66). This comparison is not a certification of every graphical or non-text difference.
- The October 2022 guide, printed pp. 25–33, supports the guide-level distinctions in the inventory. In particular, p. 26 places diminishing marginal returns and increasing marginal cost in HL supply; pp. 31–33 place market structures, firm outcomes and cost/revenue calculations in HL. The inventory compresses teaching prerequisites: teach what marginal and average costs and revenue mean, their calculation from data, and their use in firm diagrams and profit outcomes; distinguish these prerequisite explanations from separately listed mandatory guide diagrams. The checked pages do not themselves establish that a standalone complete production-and-cost-curve unit, every AVC shutdown condition, or price discrimination is required; do not assert those are excluded from all possible assessment either.
- Guide pp. 32–33 expressly identify specific perfect-competition, monopoly, collusive oligopoly and monopolistic-competition diagrams and outcomes, including welfare loss, natural monopoly, and a simple payoff matrix. The inventory's general “diagram firm outcomes” is compressed; authors should check each named outcome individually. A topic list by itself is not evidence of complete AO and diagram coverage.

## Exam-paper evidence

The IB's official [public sample-paper page](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/sample-exam-papers/) lists no economics paper in the page inspected. An openly indexed third-party copy of [IB Economics HL/SL specimen papers for first examinations 2022](https://rodrisy.github.io/ibresources/docs/econspec2022.pdf) was inspected through the web reader. Its cover and paper headers identify the course and IB copyright; the host's authorization and identity are unverified. Public accessibility alone does not establish permission to redistribute it. Findings below describe structure and transferable skills only and do not reproduce question text or extracts. This is evidence of one specimen, not a representative survey of past examinations.

- HL Paper 1, printed pp. 1–2: 1 hour 15 minutes; select one of three extended-response questions; each pairs a 10-mark explanation with a 15-mark evaluation/discussion requiring real-world examples. No calculator; diagrams where appropriate. One specimen question pairs distinct microeconomic topics; practice should not assume every pair stays inside one subtopic.
- HL Paper 2, printed pp. 1–10: 1 hour 45 minutes; select one of two data-response questions, 40 marks; use extracts and numerical material, quantitative working, diagrams and contextual reference. The [IB examiner instructions](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=2&doc=EX_instructions_2022_e&part=11) specify seven parts and explain that the final response uses the stimulus plus economic analysis and evaluation. Practice should include definitions, calculations, data interpretation, contextual diagrams/explanations and longer reasoned evaluation.
- HL Paper 3 specimen, printed pp. 1–13: 1 hour 45 minutes; answer both questions for 60 marks; a calculator and shown working are expected. Text/data, calculations and diagrams feed into an evidence-based recommendation. The IB examiner instructions specify two 30-mark questions, with up to 20 marks for part (a) and 10 for the policy recommendation in part (b). This is the ordinary structure evidenced by the specimen and instructions, not an assurance of unchanged May 2028 arrangements.
- The actual 2022 session had a temporary COVID adaptation: [IB's 2022 adaptation notice](https://www.ibo.org/contentassets/5895a05412144fe890312bad52b17044/may-and-november-2022-adapted-assessments.pdf), p. 1, says candidates chose one of two HL Paper 3 questions. Do not infer standard timing or choice from 2022 past papers without accounting for that adaptation.

For later practice, obtain authorized specimen/past papers and markschemes through the school or IB and check session-specific rules. Produce original questions, data and scoring guidance; do not copy or closely paraphrase protected papers or claim that an original exercise is an official IB question. The open specimen observed here is sufficient to describe its own structure, but it does not establish distribution rights, current-session amendments, complete topic frequency, or the content of other past papers.
